Resume creation guidelines

  1. Keep it concise: students and freshers can usually aim for one page.
  2. Use clear sections: Summary, Education, Skills, Projects, Experience and Certifications.
  3. Show proof: describe what you built, which tools you used and what you achieved.
  4. Use action words: developed, designed, analysed, tested, organised.
  5. Tailor it: include skills that honestly match the job description.
  6. Check carefully: verify spelling, dates, links and contact details before applying.
  7. Be truthful: never add skills, marks, certificates or experience you do not have.
Tip: For a project, explain the problem, your contribution, technologies used and outcome. Do not just write the project title.
